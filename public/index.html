Student Sovereignty & Privacy FAQ

How your academic data remains exclusively yours

Can college administrators or professors view my attendance or notes? expand_more

No. There is zero administrative access. College OS is completely independent and owned exclusively by you. All attendance records, grade simulations, and vault files remain strictly within your authenticated account.

How does the Nuclear Factory Reset work? expand_more

Under Profile & Settings ➔ Preferences, clicking Factory Reset wipes all your saved academic records (subjects, timetable slots, attendance marks, homework, and vault files) on both your current device cache and on the Firestore cloud, restoring a pristine default workspace.
